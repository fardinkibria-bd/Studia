# 📚 Studia

> **A modern study planning and productivity platform built specifically for students.**

Studia is a student-focused productivity platform designed to make everyday studying more organized, manageable, and productive.

Instead of forcing students to use separate apps for homework, revision, routines, study sessions, tasks, and progress tracking, Studia brings these essential parts of academic life together in one centralized workspace.

The goal is simple: **spend less time managing your studies and more time actually studying.**

## 🎯 Why Studia?

Students often manage their academic life across multiple disconnected tools:

* Notes for planning
* Calendar apps for deadlines
* Reminder apps for tasks
* Timers for studying
* Spreadsheets for tracking progress
* Separate tools for productivity

This fragmentation creates unnecessary friction.

**Studia brings the workflow together.**

The platform is designed around the way students actually work, giving them one place to plan, execute, and evaluate their studies.

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

Studia is built around three simple principles:

### 1. Plan

Know what needs to be done.

### 2. Study

Focus on completing the work instead of constantly organizing it.

### 3. Improve

Use your activity and progress data to understand your habits and make better decisions.
